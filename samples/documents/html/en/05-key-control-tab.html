5. Key & Control Tab

A fifth tab in the monitor app for the everyday path of a first-time user: get a key, enroll it in the car, then control the car with buttons. The Control tab stays as the diagnostic screen where every call is a separate button; this tab hides the order of calls behind four steps and a grid of buttons.

Built on Android and iOS, real-device check pending

Android build 0.4.0 (4) and iOS build 0.5.0 (26). The step rules and the command runner are covered by unit tests; the screen and the car are checked with the checklist at the end of this page. The Infotainment section (SFR-025) is built on both.

Steps and when buttons are enabled

The rules are one pure function (KeyControlSteps), so they are tested without a car. Its inputs: the key is ready, the key is locked by the phone lock, a VIN is saved, the app is connected with a key (Connected and vehicle.hasKey; a key-store error at connect time connects without a key; step 2 then stays "to do" and its button becomes Disconnect and connect again, which stops the monitor's connection and connects again with the key), the enrollment state, and whether a call of this tab is running.

Enrollment state

The enrollment state is one of unknown, checking, notEnrolled and enrolled.

  • The tab checks with listKeys(): it looks for the car's entry whose public key equals the local key (manual chapter 13). During the check the state is checking; afterwards enrolled or notEnrolled, or unknown when the result is Uncertain or Failure. The check runs once each time the tab sees a new connection with a ready key (retried once after about 5 seconds when it ends unknown), each time the tab becomes visible, and when Check enrollment is pressed. On iOS every one of these checks, manual ones included, first waits for the monitor's first query round after a connect to end, so the two listKeys() do not run at the same time and share one time budget on a slow connect. While it waits the tab is busy, so its other buttons are refused for up to about 15 seconds (three queries of up to 5 seconds each). The check can still end unknown and be retried, for example when another listKeys() runs at the same time: the Control tab's manual List keys button does not wait and can still overlap a poll or a check. An automatic check never writes a "connect first" line.
  • It becomes enrolled when the pairing confirmation succeeds: sessionInfo(publicKey, INFOTAINMENT) every 3 seconds for up to 2 minutes. As in the manual's waitUntilEnrolled, polling continues on KeyNotPaired and Uncertain and stops at once on any other failure (for example NotConnected after the link drops).
  • It is checked again when the Control tab confirms an enrollment or removes this device's key.
  • It becomes notEnrolled when the session before a command, or the command itself (the session was already Ready, so nothing was sent for it), fails with KeyNotPaired.
  • It goes back to unknown when the connection is lost or the key changes.

It shows the existing flow (addKeyRequest, then the confirmation from manual chapter 13 "Enrolling a key") as three rows and one message. Built on iOS; Android: planned, same spec. The Control tab's Request enrollment keeps its result lines and has no popup.

  • Blocking. The sheet cannot be swiped away while the flow runs; only Cancel (and Close once it has ended).
  • Cancel really stops the waiting. It cancels the task of the flow: the request collection and the 3-second check loop end (a sessionInfo call already in flight ends within its 5-second timeout). After pressing it the sheet says it is stopping, then shows the cancelled state. The step 3 state is not changed by cancelling.
  • What cancelling does not undo. The request already sent to the car cannot be recalled. The car may still show its approval prompt, and a key card tap afterwards can still enroll the key. The cancelled sheet says so and points to Check enrollment.
  • Dismissed when the app has moved on. Any change of the connection (link loss, reconnect) or of the key closes the sheet at once, stops the flow, and adds a result line ("enrollment waiting was stopped"); updates that arrive afterwards are dropped. A finished sheet (done, not confirmed, failed, cancelled) also goes away in that case.
  • Step and state logic is pure: EnrollmentPopup (phases, rows, texts, button rules, remaining time), EnrollmentUpdate (what the flow reports), EnrollmentWaiter.wait(onAttempt:); tests in Tests/EnrollmentPopupTests.swift. The sheet in Views/KeyControlTab.swift only draws it.

Delete and recreate the key

Step 1 has a second button, Delete and recreate the key, that replaces the monitor key teslable-monitor with a new one (for example after the key was removed from the car for good, or the stored key is corrupted). Built on iOS; Android: planned, same spec. Library manual: chapter 13 "Deleting or replacing a key locally" and "Key accessibility", chapter 17 "Session cache".

RuleDetail
EnabledNo other call runs (this tab's, or the Control tab's pairing and commands: otherwise the result says "busy, press again later" and nothing changes), and either the key is ready and not locked, or the stored key is corrupted (next rows). Any other key error (not read yet, another keys.get failure) keeps it off. A connection is not needed.
ConfirmationAlways, before anything changes. It says: the old key is removed from this phone permanently and cannot be restored; the car still lists the old public key until it is removed on the car (its screen, or removeKey from another enrolled key); the new key is not enrolled, so it must be enrolled again with the key card (step 3); the connection is dropped and made again.
LockedThe key store is read first (keys.get(alias)). KeyStoreError.Locked changes nothing, not even the connection, and the result says to unlock the phone (the manual: never delete or re-create on Locked). A Locked during the delete restores the old key and reconnects; a Locked during the create counts as a failed create (no retry).
Corrupted keyKeyStoreError.PlatformFailure whose cause is exactly stored key is corrupted (an exact match, never a substring: the library puts "key store failure: " in front of it in message, and a false positive would delete a healthy enrolled key after the confirmation) (manual chapter 13 "Deleting or replacing a key locally", chapter 16, chapter 17 "Key storage") is the one documented damage case. The key state becomes corrupted, step 1 says so, the button is on, and the read gate accepts it (the key cannot be read, that is the point): delete, then create. The confirmation then also says the damaged old key cannot be recovered. The library never replaces a corrupted key by itself; the app only does it after this confirmation.
Other tabs and racing connectsAn engine-level gate (KeyOperationGate) covers every tab. Replace is refused (busy) while a Control-tab call runs; Control-tab calls are refused while the key is replaced; connectNow, the automatic connect, the unlock retry and ensureKey() do nothing during the replacement, so a connect pressed on Live or Control cannot take the old handle. The user's last press wins (SFR-012): a Connect pressed during the replacement is remembered and honoured when the replacement ends with a usable key (success, or the old key unchanged), also when no connection was running; a Disconnect or Clear VIN pressed meanwhile cancels the reconnect, so a stop is never undone. If the replacement ends without a usable key state (failed create, lost, unverified, unknownLocked), nothing connects and the result says so. The key handle cache has a generation: a read that finishes after the handle was dropped is not cached, so the deleted key never stays cached. After the disconnect the replacer checks that no connection remains; if one does, nothing is deleted. replaced is therefore never reported with an old-key connection.
Order (KeyReplacer, pure)Read the key store, disconnect (only if the monitor connection was running: connecting, connected or waiting to retry; a connection the user stopped stays stopped), drop the key handle, keys.delete(alias), keys.getOrCreate(alias, PREFER_HARDWARE, AFTER_FIRST_UNLOCK) with the same settings as step 1, then reconnect with the new key (if the link was running or Connect was pressed meanwhile, and no Disconnect after that).
Why the connection goes firstThe live Vehicle, its sessions and the session cache belong to the old key (the cache is keyed by the SHA-1 of the public key). After a key change the next connect discards the old cache and handshakes again, so the old connection is not kept.
Failed deleteThe new key is not created. The old key is read again (keys.get, never getOrCreate) and the result follows what was read: the key is there, so the old key is unchanged and the connection is made again if it was running; the key is gone anyway (lost), so nothing is created and there is no reconnect (a connect would create a key silently); the state cannot be read (unverified), so there is no reconnect either and the result says to press step 1 "Reload"; the key store is locked after the failed delete, so nobody knows whether the key was deleted (unknownLocked): no reconnect either (after the unlock a connect would create a key silently), and the result says the state is unknown until the phone is unlocked and step 1 "Reload" is pressed.
Failed createThe old key is gone and there is no new key. No silent retry and no reconnect; the result says to press step 1.
After successStep 3 goes back to "not enrolled" (a new key is not on the car; the check after reconnecting confirms it). The status card and the enrollment progress lines are cleared, because they came from the old key's connection. The Control tab key list and key info are cleared.
NicknameA key nickname (SFR-019) belongs to the old key id and stays with it; it is not moved to the new key.

Running a command

KeyControlRunner.run(ensureSession, command, requery) is pure and tested with fakes:

  1. ensureSession is startSession(setOf(VCSEC)). A domain that is already Ready succeeds without a request. On Failure (or Uncertain) the command is not sent and nothing is re-queried and the result line says why; KeyNotPaired sets the enrollment to notEnrolled, and KeyStoreError.Locked asks to unlock the phone.
  2. command is called exactly once.
  3. Success shows "succeeded", Failure shows "failed" with the message, Uncertain shows "uncertain" with the message and is never resent. When the error is Timeout, a sentence from 16-lib-errors.md (Timeout(afterSend) row) follows the message: afterSend = true says it may have been sent and the car may have run it, so read the state again, and notes that BLE writes can be slow in the background; afterSend = false says it was not executed (no write had started, the wait for a retry ran out, or the handshake timed out). The re-query follows either way.
  4. requery (vehicleStatus()) always runs after the command: after Success it refreshes the status card, after Uncertain it is the re-query the manual requires. A failed re-query is added to the same result line.

Confirming a dialog checks the step 4 rule again; if it no longer holds, or another call is running, nothing is sent and a result line says why. The Model X and Cybertruck buttons sit in a collapsed "Other models" section. The Infotainment climate and charging buttons are a separate section below the grid (Infotainment controls); the other Infotainment commands (horn, lights, windows, media) wait for the rest of M5 and are not shown. The tab also has a status card (lock, trunk, frunk, sleep and the time of the last read) with a Refresh status button, and a result list with the newest line first, time stamped and without the VIN. The status card belongs to one connection: it is cleared when the connection changes, and a re-query that ends after the connection changed does not update it.

All library calls of this tab run one at a time through the tab's own exclusive { }, and the buttons are disabled while a call runs. On Android they run in the view model's scope, so switching tabs or rotating the phone does not cancel them. The Control tab has its own guard; the library serializes VCSEC commands per connection, so presses on both tabs cannot interleave on the wire.

Callouts and actions (SFR-024, iOS). On a wide screen (iPhone Duo inner display) the right pane shows the lock icon centred above the car photo, Frunk open (small "Open" over bold "Front") top left, Trunk open/close ("Open/Close" over bold "Trunk") top right, and a bottom row with Wake up and Close trunk (QuickControls). They call the same KeyControlModel.press, so every rule on this page applies unchanged: step 4 must be on (otherwise they are dimmed and the same orange hint shows once), Frunk open asks first, a session is ensured, the command is sent once, vehicleStatus() follows, and Uncertain is never resent. The right pane shows the confirmation dialog while this tab is open in the left pane (ownsCommandDialog: false here), so there is only one dialog. The latest result line appears under the car.

When the buttons are enabled

The six buttons use exactly the step 4 rule: key ready, connected with a key (Vehicle.hasKey), enrollment enrolled, and no library call of this tab running. There is no separate rule and no cached "session is ready" input: the tap starts the INFOTAINMENT session, so the user never starts a session first. A tap whose conditions no longer hold when it runs sends nothing, and the result line says why (the same text as for the VCSEC buttons).

Order of one tap

It follows 15-lib-commands.md "Call order" and 14-lib-reading-state.md A8.2, and is the same order as SFR-023:

  1. vehicleStatus() with the default timeout. Only sleepStatus == AWAKE goes on. ASLEEP, UNKNOWN or a failed or Uncertain read ends the tap as NotSent: no session is started, nothing is sent, and wakeUp() is never called. Waking is the user's decision: the Wake up button of the Doors group, then wait for AWAKE, then tap again. A successful read refreshes the status card.
  2. startSession(setOf(INFOTAINMENT)) with the default timeout. A domain that is already Ready succeeds without a request. Failure or Uncertain ends the tap as NotSent; KeyNotPaired also sets the enrollment to notEnrolled, and KeyStoreError.Locked asks to unlock the phone.
  3. One command call with the default timeout (null). Never a second one, whatever the result.
  4. Nothing else: no automatic getState and no re-query chain. The user reads the state with the read buttons.

A read tap is the same, with getState(category) as step 3.

For a read tap, Success shows the readout; Uncertain and Failure are shown as the library returned them (16-lib-errors.md "getState results"), the last readout stays with its age, and there is no automatic retry.

  • Never resend. The app never calls a command a second time by itself, not after Uncertain and not after a read. The buttons stay enabled while a category is unconfirmed (the user decides), but the line says to read first.
  • Unconfirmed marker. After an Uncertain command the section shows "climate result unconfirmed" or "charge result unconfirmed" with the matching read button next to it. The marker of a category is cleared when a getState of that category returns Success, when a later command of that category returns Success, or when the connection changes. An Uncertain read keeps it. Other failures of a command neither set nor clear it.

Readout

Values are shown on screen only (FR-112): the last successful read per category, its time and age, and the reply's timestamp. Only these named fields are read; a missing field shows as a dash and 0 stays 0 (14-lib-reading-state.md A8.3).

Climateis_climate_on, inside_temp_celsius, driver_temp_setting, timestamp

If the timestamp of a read equals the one of the previous read of that category, the readout says the timestamp did not change, so it is not necessarily a new measurement (A8.3). The readout uses charger_power as the visible sign of charging and does not show charge_state.charging_state yet. The library manual now describes how to read that oneof wrapper (14-lib-reading-state.md A8.6); showing it in the samples is a follow-up.

Connection change and late results

The readouts, the unconfirmed markers and the result of a tap belong to the connection (the Vehicle instance) that produced them. When the connection changes (link loss, reconnect, key change) the readouts and markers are cleared. A result that arrives after the connection changed is dropped: no readout, no marker, no update of the enrollment or the status card. One result line records it and names the command only: the result was dropped because the connection changed, the car may have run it, read the state after reconnecting.

Log and result lines

One result line per finished tap and nothing else (no observe line, no file log): the button label, the result, the error type in parentheses (unless Success) and the duration, for example <climate on label>: <uncertain word> (Timeout(afterSend=true)) 5012 ms. Uncertain and NotSent add one fixed sentence. No value, no field name, no vehicle text and no error.message goes into the line (FR-112, 14-lib-reading-state.md A8.3 "Do not log a result"). The error types are ResponseTooLarge (ProtocolFault(RESPONSE_MTU_EXCEEDED)), Timeout(afterSend=true|false), NoSession, and otherwise the class name of the error (for example InfotainmentRejected, NotConnected, Locked). The duration is the whole tap, from the first library call.

One call at a time

Every call runs through the tab's own exclusive { }: one at a time, all buttons off while busy. A second tap while one runs does nothing and logs "busy". The library does not serialize Infotainment calls, so this guard is the only one.

Android: InfotainmentControl.kt (pure: InfoCommand, InfotainmentRunner, InfotainmentErrors, the InfoUiState reducer and the readout mapping) with src/test/.../InfotainmentControlTest.kt; the state is in KeyControlState and the screen in ui/KeyControlTab.kt. iOS: InfotainmentControl.swift (pure, the same rules and sentences), InfotainmentLink.swift (the four library calls of one tap on one connection's Vehicle), KeyControlModel.pressInfo / runInfo (state, results, the connection generation that drops late results) and the section in Views/KeyControlTab.swift; tests with a fake KeyControlBackend and a fake InfotainmentLink as for SFR-021. The iOS shows the climate and charge readout with a TimelineView, so the age counts up without touching the model.

Real-car checklist

  1. Fresh install: step 1 shows "to do"; press it and the key is created, step 1 is done.
  2. VIN saved and the car nearby: step 2 is done after connecting; the enrollment check runs (checking, then notEnrolled for a new key).
  3. Step 3: press it, put the key card on the console and approve on the car's screen; progress lines appear, then enrolled, and the step 4 buttons turn on.
  4. Lock, unlock, wake up: the result is "succeeded" and the status card follows.
  5. Trunk toggle and close; frunk with the confirmation dialog.
  6. Reinstall while the key is still enrolled: after connecting, the check gives enrolled without pairing again.
  7. Remove the key on the car's screen and press a command: the session fails with KeyNotPaired and step 3 goes back to "to do".

SFR-020, delete and recreate the key (the iOS runbook is in samples/ios/README.md):

  1. Press Delete and recreate the key: the dialog states the four points (permanent removal from this phone, the car still lists the old public key, enroll again with the key card, the connection is dropped). Cancel changes nothing.
  2. Confirm while connected and enrolled: the connection drops and comes back, step 1 stays done, step 3 shows "not enrolled" after the check, the status card shows nothing from the old connection, the Control tab key list is empty. Key list then shows the old public key on the car and no row marked "this device".
  3. Enroll the new key (step 3). A nickname given to the old key id does not appear on the new key.
  4. After a restart, before the first unlock: the button is off, or the result says the key store is locked and nothing changed (no key deleted, connection unchanged).
  5. With the VIN saved but disconnected (Disconnect pressed): a new key is created and no connection is started.

SFR-020, corrupted key and other tabs (iOS runbook: samples/ios/README.md):

  1. Corrupted key (only if you can reproduce it, for example an Android restore without its wrapping key; hard on iOS): step 1 says the key is damaged and the button is on; the dialog adds that the old key cannot be recovered; after confirming, a new key exists. A key error that is not stored key is corrupted leaves the button off.
  2. Racing taps: start Request enrollment on the Control tab and press the replace button: the result says busy. Press replace and, while it runs, Connect on Live: no connection starts until the new key exists; afterwards the app reconnects with the new key (the Connect press was remembered), also if nothing was running before. Press Disconnect on Live while it runs: after the replacement the app stays disconnected.

SFR-021, enrollment progress popup (the iOS runbook is in samples/ios/README.md):

  1. Press Request enrollment: the sheet opens at once, then says the request was sent and to put the key card on the centre console and approve on the car's screen.
  2. Tap the card and approve: the sheet moves to checking with attempt number, elapsed and remaining time; then "done" and Close; step 3 is done and the step 4 buttons work.
  3. Do not approve for 2 minutes: "not confirmed in 2 minutes" with the advice; Close; Check enrollment works.
  4. Press Cancel while waiting: the sheet says it is stopping, then cancelled with the note that the request cannot be recalled; no further sessionInfo requests (Log tab). Tap the card afterwards, then Check enrollment: it may show enrolled.
  5. Turn Bluetooth off or walk away while the sheet is open: the sheet closes at once and a result line says the waiting was stopped.
  6. While the flow runs, swipe the sheet down: it stays.

SFR-025, Infotainment climate and charging (everything is unconfirmed on a real car, #118; write what the car did, not values):

  1. Awake car, enrolled key: the six Infotainment buttons are on; with Bluetooth off, or before step 3, they are off.
  2. Climate on: the result is "succeeded"; Read climate shows is_climate_on; the car's own screen agrees. Then Climate off and read again.
  3. Charge start and Charge stop with a cable connected: the result, then Read charge (charger_power, battery_level); note what the car showed. Without a cable: note the result type the car returns.
  4. Let the car fall asleep and tap Climate on: "not sent, asleep", no wake and no session (the Log tab shows no Infotainment traffic). Press Wake up, wait until the status card says awake, tap again.
  5. Force an Uncertain (for example walk to the edge of range right after the tap): the line says uncertain with the error type, the "unconfirmed" marker shows, no second command is sent, and Read climate clears it when it succeeds.
  6. Drop the link while a command runs: the late result is dropped (one "dropped" line) and the readouts are cleared.
  7. Copy the result lines: they contain command names, outcomes, durations and error types only.
